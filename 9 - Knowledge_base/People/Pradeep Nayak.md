# Pradeep Nayak

Pradeep Nayak is relevant to this PhD through his work on the shift in India from redistributive land reform toward land titling, land liberalisation, and market-oriented land governance.

## Scholarly Lens
Nayak reads land titling as a political-economic transition, not merely as an administrative upgrade. His work is useful because it places titling within the broader retreat from land reform and the rise of policies that make land more transferable, investment-ready, and legible to markets.

## Contribution to the Thesis
For this thesis, Nayak helps frame DILRMP as part of a wider policy movement: from correcting agrarian inequality toward modernising records, facilitating transactions, and making land usable for credit, investment, welfare targeting, and acquisition. This is directly relevant to the chapter on political economy of land records modernisation in India.
