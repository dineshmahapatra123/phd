# Sovereignty-Discipline-Government Triangle

## Standard Definition
The **sovereignty-discipline-government triangle** is Foucault's correction to a simple linear story in which modern government replaces sovereignty. In chapter 4, he argues that sovereignty, discipline, and government coexist: modern rule combines legal authority, disciplinary techniques, and governmental management of population through security apparatuses.

This matters because land-record modernisation should not be read as a clean transition from old revenue sovereignty to neutral digital administration. Digital land governance combines all three elements: sovereign law, disciplinary documentation, and governmental calculation.

## Scholarly Debate
- **Foucault (1991)**: Modern power is not only command over territory. It is also management of population and things through economy, statistics, police, and security.
- **[[State Legibility]] tradition**: Scott's account of cadastral maps shows how governmental calculation becomes material: maps and records make land actionable from the centre.
- **Technocratic reform literature**: Often treats digitisation as administrative improvement. The triangle shows why digitisation can simultaneously improve service delivery, discipline claimants into formal categories, and strengthen state capacity over land.

## PhD Application
For the thesis, the triangle is a compact way to explain DILRMP's layered character:

- **Sovereignty**: land law, title presumptions, state authority over revenue records, acquisition, and dispute institutions
- **Discipline**: standardised formats, plot numbers, khata numbers, mutation procedures, authentication, and document compliance
- **Government**: population management, welfare targeting, credit enablement, market integration, risk reduction, planning, and data interoperability

This directly serves Chapter 2.1 and Chapter 3.4. It also helps avoid an oversimplified claim that DILRMP is merely a colonial revenue inheritance. The better argument is that DILRMP digitises a colonial inheritance and redeploys it inside a postcolonial governmental apparatus.

## Measurement / Methods
- Trace how one land parcel moves through law, record format, office procedure, and welfare/market databases
- Compare the legal status of RoR with its practical use as an eligibility and verification instrument
- Observe how citizens are required to discipline their claims into state-readable documents before accessing correction, mutation, or benefits
