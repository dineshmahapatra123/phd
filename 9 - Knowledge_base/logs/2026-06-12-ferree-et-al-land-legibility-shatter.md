1. Scott: the state makes society legible.

This creates a four-part distinction for the thesis: documentary legibility, issuing authority, institutional recognition, and correspondence with possession and ground reality.

## Caveats
- The experiment measures perceived security and purchase desirability, not actual legal enforcement, investment, conflict, or dispossession.
- The control condition omitted information about documents rather than specifying their absence.
- Telephone sampling overrepresents more connected and educated respondents.
- The named-purchaser vignette cannot measure losses imposed on omitted heirs, tenants, secondary-rights holders, or rival claimants.
