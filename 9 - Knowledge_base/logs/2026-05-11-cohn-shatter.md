## What Was Deliberately Excluded

The following content from Cohn was read and assessed, then excluded:

- **Command of Language chapter**: Orientalist philology, language teaching for colonial administration (Carey, Gilchrist, Jones, Halhed). Relevant to general colonial knowledge-power theory but does not map to any specific chapter section on land administration, land records, or property. Excluded per Test 3.
- **Transformation of Objects chapter**: Mackenzie Collection, Amaravati marbles, museological practice. Archaeological collecting is not in scope. Excluded per Test 3.
- **Cloth and Colonialism chapter**: Sikh turban, colonial dress codes, identity politics. No connection to land administration or DILRMP. Excluded per Tests 1 and 3.
