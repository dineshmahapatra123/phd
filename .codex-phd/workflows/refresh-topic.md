# Codex Workflow: refresh-topic

Purpose: update a living topic synthesis from the current knowledge base.

## Steps

1. Locate the requested note in `9 - Knowledge_base/Topics/`.
2. Read `PHD_LENS.md`, the topic note, `index.md`, and relevant notes from `Concepts/`, `People/`, `Methods/`, and `sources/`.
3. Identify new evidence or contradictions since the topic was last processed.
4. Integrate only evidence that passes the `PHD_LENS.md` relevance tests or directly serves the topic's mapped thesis chapter/debate.
5. Append or update the synthesis while preserving older useful evidence.
6. Use `### ⚠️ Contradiction` for conflicting findings.
7. Follow `PHD_CONSTITUTION.md` -> Operational Logging when the refresh materially changes the KB.
8. Update `Last_Processed` to the current date.
9. Summarize what changed, including what was skipped as outside the PhD lens when relevant.
