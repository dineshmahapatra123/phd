> "the new land titles given were, largely, a derivative of the existing presumptive titles" (p. 748)

> "a large plot of land is jointly owned by more than two brothers, but each of them cultivates distinct parcels as if they own them" (p. 735)

### Concept: Welfare targeting

> "Rythu Bandhu is meant for farmers. It is not tenant Bandhu" (p. 750)
